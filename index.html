01 · The Polymath Question
What makes you want to build like a polymath?
— 100 word answer —
Honestly, I don't think in single lanes. I'll spend a night tuning a Dataflow pipeline and the next wondering if it could actually be sold to a shop owner who's never heard the word pipeline. That gap is where I like living. Building CrowdIQ taught me the tech isn't the hard part — figuring out who'd pay for it is. Lately I've been sketching a small AI tool for local shops near NMIT to handle inventory and customer queries without extra hires. It's not live yet, but I keep coming back to it. That's the entrepreneur in me talking — I don't just want to build, I want to build things people need.
